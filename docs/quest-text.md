## The Ranger's books

### The Ranger's Trail Book (by Ranger Juniper)

Page 1: Ten winds sleep along the old trails. Long ago each one blew a spirit coat onto a critter's back, one color at a time.

Page 2: The first wind has stirred. Nine more are napping. Each wakes for a wrangler who is kind to critters and patient on the trail.

Page 3: Look for the wind-signs. They stand where the grass is soft and the sky is wide. Be kind to them, and to each other.

### The Watch-Bell (by Ranger Juniper)

Page 1: A bell hangs at the Ranger's watch. It rings when the night is long and a friend is near.

Page 2: Hold the line, little wrangler. Keep your critter close. The dark is only dark, and the dawn is on its way.

Page 3: Thank you for standing the watch. The Ranger sleeps easier tonight.

### The Meadow Book (by Ranger Juniper)

Page 1: Past the hill, the lake and the river, the flowers grow. That is the Far Meadow, where the herd comes home.

Page 2: The meadow sign says only: the wrangler was here. The meadow keeps no names. It keeps every friend.

### The Ten Winds Are Awake (by Ranger Juniper)

Page 1: The winds have woken, one by one, and the fog is thinning. Juniper's trails sing again.

Page 2: Every trail you walked helped, a little: a sign stood up, a ring glowed, a bell rang, a meadow was found.

Page 3: Thank you, wrangler. Bring your herd home whenever you like. The meadow is yours.
